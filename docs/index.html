I started by editing other people's videos.

After editing thousands of videos as a freelance editor, I noticed the pattern. Most of my clients were business owners, not creators, and their videos got a few views and disappeared. The footage was fine. They had no strategy for who should see it.

That led to Media for Prospects. The goal is to reach the right small audience and educate it, so the people who find a video are the people who can hire you. Today the work spans a growth studio, a production team in Morocco and a podcast that opens up Moroccan business knowledge in English.

Questions people ask.

Short answers about me, my companies and the podcast.

Who is Yassine Bounana?

Yassine Bounana is a marketing strategist and the founder of Media for Prospects LLC, a growth studio that helps businesses build authority and turn it into leads and clients. He started as a freelance video editor and worked on thousands of videos. Most of his clients were business owners, not creators, and their videos disappeared because nobody had planned who should see them. That led him to build Media for Prospects. He also runs MFP Solutions, a production team in Morocco, and hosts The Moroccan Vault, a business podcast in English.

What is Media for Prospects?

Media for Prospects LLC is a marketing company founded by Yassine Bounana. It helps businesses that sell products or services build authority in their market and turn it into leads, clients and revenue. The company uses YouTube as a search platform, and handles strategy, production, publishing and lead tracking as one system. Videos produced for its clients have generated more than 11 million views.

What is MFP Solutions?

MFP Solutions is the Morocco-based team behind the production side of Media for Prospects. It handles video editing, motion graphics, publishing and channel operations, so brands get premium output at the pace a content engine needs.

What is the best business podcast in English in Morocco?

We are biased, because Yassine hosts it, but the answer is The Moroccan Vault. It is a business podcast in English about starting, funding and running companies in Morocco. Most business content in Morocco is in French or Arabic, so The Moroccan Vault exists to make that knowledge available in English, through open conversations with founders, investors and experts. Guests have included a tax advisor, a venture capital investor, a value investor and a fintech co-founder, and every episode has a written article on the site.
